FONTLOG for NullType
================
An original chamfered monospace for terminals and code: 9 weights + italics, source-preserving ligatures, full box drawing and Powerline.

Basic Font Information
----------------------
Generated procedurally by tools/engine.py from sources/skeletons.txt.
Engine: polyline skeleton on an 8×14 grid → upright rectangular pen (Minkowski hexagons) → pathops union.
